# Project: Twickenham Cranial Osteopath Website

---

**External User’s Goal:** The user is a local visitor experiencing non-urgent musculoskeletal issues who wants clear, reassuring information about how cranial osteopathy works, what to expect at a first appointment, and the practitioner’s credentials. They seek an easy-to-navigate, trustworthy site that helps them feel confident taking the next step toward booking an appointment.

**Site Owner’s Goal:** The site owner is a newly established, GOsC-registered cranial osteopath in Twickenham aiming to build a local client base. She wants a clean, welcoming multi-page website (minimum three distinct pages, each designed to showcase distinct layouts and content) that communicates her expertise and appointment-based services clearly, helping local visitors feel confident getting in touch while demonstrating strong accessibility, responsiveness, and structured design using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap.

**Potential Features might Include:**

- **Navigation & Hero Section:** Include a responsive Bootstrap navigation bar linking the site’s pages, paired with a welcoming hero banner introducing the Twickenham practice, a calming background image or colour, and a clear call-to-action button.
- **Practitioner Bio & Credentials:** Organise a dedicated section using Bootstrap’s grid system to introduce the osteopath, highlight her GOsC registration, and build trust with prospective clients.
- **Treatment Cards:** Use Bootstrap’s card components in a responsive grid to present key offerings (such as cranial osteopathy, initial consultations, and follow-up appointments) in a clear, scannable format.
- **FAQ Section:** Present common questions (such as what to expect and how appointment-based visits work) using Bootstrap’s accordion or list group component for easy reading.
- **Contact & Enquiry Section:** Include an accessible contact form styled with Bootstrap’s form components—featuring clear validation, immediate submission feedback, and prominent action buttons—so visitors can easily send an enquiry to arrange an appointment.
