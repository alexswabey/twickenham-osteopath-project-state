# Osteopathy Website Benchmarks — Meadowside Clinic

> Reference audit of **6 osteopathy & clinic websites** guiding Meadowside Clinic's tone, layout, and UX decisions.

## Core Design Takeaways

1. **Calm warmth over generic clinical blue:** Pair light neutral/warm surfaces with restrained clinical accents rather than standard corporate blues/bright teals (*Maris*, *Body Evolve*).
2. **Practitioner & trust upfront:** Lead early with the solo practitioner's name, portrait, and GOsC credentials (*Sora*, *Wellthy*, *Evolution*) — never an anonymous *"About me"* page (*Body Evolve*).
3. **Clear boundaries & appointment expectations:** Address first-timer hesitation directly using *"What we aren't"* framing (*Sora*), clear visit steps (*Body Evolve*), and practical clothing/consent/chaperone details (*Maris*).
4. **Restrained nav & low-pressure CTAs:** Favour 4–5 clear nav items and scannable card tags (*Muna*) plus phone/enquiry options (*Wellthy*, *Sora*) over mega-menus or 14-link headers (*Maris*, *Body Evolve*).
5. **Clean execution over heavy clutter:** Avoid dense therapy walls, duplicated intros, unfinished template placeholders, and broken/empty `alt` text.

## Site Reference (6 Clinics)

| Clinic | Location / Focus | Aesthetic & Feel | Emulate | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[Wellthy Clinic](https://wellthyclinic.com)** | Marylebone, W1 · osteopathy, cranial, paediatric, Pilates | Crisp neutrals (`#F9F9F9`, charcoal, olive-grey, midnight) · `Outfit` / `Inter` | Symptom-to-cause hero (*"Treating the cause not just the symptoms"*), cranial/paediatric nav labels, early team & review proof, Contact / Call / Book choice | Heavy page weight, missing `alt` text |
| **[Muna Wellness](https://www.munawellness.com.au)** | Australia · osteopathy, clinical Pilates, massage | Soft mint washes, slate ink, warm coral · geometric sans | Ultra-focused 4-item nav, short upbeat hero (*"Heal. Move. Grow."*), scannable availability/mode card tags, airy mobile rhythm | Multi-clinic location switchers |
| **[Sora Health](https://sorahealth.co.uk)** | Cambridgeshire · solo osteopath (Holly Matthews) | Warm ivory, stone greige, slate-mist · serif (`Ovo`) + sans (`Mulish`) | Practitioner right below hero, *"What we aren't"* (*"No Quick Cracks"*), warm chat CTAs (*"Call for a Friendly Chat"*), FAQ + testimonials above closing CTA | Heavy builder assets, missing `alt` text |
| **[The Maris Practice](https://themarispractice.com/)** | Twickenham, TW1 · multidisciplinary & cranial/paediatric | White/slate + corporate clinical blue (`#1A80B6`) · `Zilla Slab` + `Open Sans` | Cranial/paediatric depth, thorough *"What to expect"* guide (clothing, consent, chaperones, confidentiality), calm local mews directions | 14-item nav, 16-link therapy wall, repeated intro copy, buried phone number, leaked post timestamps/staging links |
| **[Body Evolve Clinic](https://bodyevolveclinic.co.uk/)** | SW London · osteopathy, massage, acupuncture | White + deep navy + bright teal (`#0EA0A0`) · sans + script | Empathetic *"pain shrinking your world"* hook, 3-step roadmap (*Identify -> Reduce Pain -> Rebuild Strength*), *"free 15-minute phone chat"* | Condition mega-menus, unnamed *"About me"* page, live template placeholders (*"Google review code to be added here"*), missing `<nav>`/`alt`s |
| **[Evolution Osteopathy](https://www.evolutionosteopathy.co.uk/)** | Waterloo, SE1 · osteopathy & manual therapy | Geometric sans (`Familjen Grotesk` + `Ropa Sans`) | Above-the-fold trust checklist (GOsC, insurance, ratings) directly above CTA, transparent on-page logistics/FAQs, clear specialist matching | Duplicate headings/navs, unused `/cart (0)` header clutter |
